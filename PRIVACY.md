# Looker privacy policy

*Last updated: 18 November 2026*

Looker is a photo viewer and file explorer for Windows. It contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or
tracking. Free Looker sends nothing about you anywhere. Looker Pro, the paid upgrade, checks its licence online, as
described below.

## What stays on your PC

- **Your files.** Looker opens the files and folders you choose and never uploads them anywhere.
- **Settings, history and search.** Your preferences, window position, recent files and folders, bookmarks, tags,
  and Looker Pro's search index of your drives' file names are saved in Looker's own app data folder on your PC.
  Settings > Reset Looker clears them; uninstalling Looker removes all of it.

## Network use

Looker connects to the internet only for these:

- **Updates.** The Microsoft Store version asks the Microsoft Store through Windows (Microsoft's
  [privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) covers that). The GitHub version downloads a
  small file from Looker's GitHub releases page that names the latest version (GitHub's
  [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) covers
  that). Neither request includes anything about you or your files.
- **Looker Pro's licence.** Only once you enter a Looker Pro key, Looker sends the licence server at veil.page:
  the key; a code made from this PC's Windows ID (a one-way hash, so the ID itself can't be read back); a code made
  from the PC's hardware IDs (also a one-way hash); Windows, the processor type and Looker's version. It does this
  when you activate, about once a week to renew, and when you deactivate. The server keeps your licence, the
  hashed codes of the PCs it's active on, their Windows and Looker versions and when they last checked in, so you
  can see and free them from your account. It also keeps a one-way code of the network the request came from, to
  spot a key shared beyond its two PCs. It never receives your name, your files, your folder names or what you do
  in Looker. The licence and its key are stored on your PC, encrypted for your Windows account.

Files in cloud folders (such as OneDrive) may be downloaded by Windows when you open them; that is handled by
Windows and your cloud provider, not by Looker.

## Buying Looker Pro

Looker Pro is sold on veil.page through Paddle (paddle.com), the reseller and merchant of record, which handles the
payment, your billing details and the receipt under its own [privacy notice](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy).
The licence server receives your email address from Paddle to send your key and to show it in your account on
veil.page, where you sign in with that email. The key, the email and the order number are kept for as long as the
licence exists.
