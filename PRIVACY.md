# Looker privacy policy

*Last updated: 6 October 2026*

Looker is a photo viewer for Windows. It does not collect, store, or send any personal information, and it contains
no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or accounts.

## What stays on your PC

- **Your photos.** Looker opens the files you choose and never uploads them anywhere.
- **Settings and recent photos.** Your preferences, window position, and the list of recently viewed photos are
  saved in Looker's own app data folder on your PC. You can turn the recent list off, or clear everything with
  Settings > Reset Looker; uninstalling Looker removes all of it.

## Network use

Looker connects to the internet only to check whether a newer version is available:

- **Microsoft Store version:** it asks the Microsoft Store through Windows. Microsoft's own
  [privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) covers that request.
- **GitHub version:** it downloads a small file from Looker's GitHub releases page that names the latest version.
  GitHub's [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
  covers that request.

No information about you or your photos is included in either request.

Photos stored in cloud folders (such as OneDrive) may be downloaded by Windows when you open them; that is handled
by Windows and your cloud provider, not by Looker.
